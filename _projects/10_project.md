[Raftar Formula Racing](https://www.linkedin.com/company/raftar-formula-racing/) is IIT Madras's Formula Student team - one of the most competitive student engineering teams in India. I spent three years here, growing from a first-year recruit to Team Captain, across two competition cars and one complete powertrain transition.

---

## What is Formula Student?

Formula Student (FSAE) is an international collegiate engineering competition where student teams design, build, and race open-wheel formula-style cars. Teams are judged across static events (engineering design, cost, business presentation) and dynamic events (acceleration, skidpad, autocross, endurance). It is one of the most rigorous applied engineering challenges at the undergraduate level.

**Frame Design & Simulation**
- Designed the space frame chassis from scratch in **Autodesk Inventor**, integrating geometric constraints from all subsystems (suspension, powertrain, ergonomics, aero)
- Iterated across multiple designs using **ANSYS** structural simulations - evaluating torsional stiffness, load paths, and safety factors
- Developed jigs and fixtures for chassis fabrication to maintain tight dimensional tolerances during welding
- Contributed to the full-car CAD assembly (~900 parts)

**Composites**
- Designed carbon fiber components (wings, body panels, bell cranks) - determined ply count and layup sequence using Classical Laminate Theory and hand calculations
- Manufactured all composite parts in-house using wet layup; achieved consistent surface finish and structural targets
- Replaced aluminum bell crank with a carbon fiber-honeycomb sandwich design - **60% weight reduction** with equivalent strength

## Team Captain - RFR22 Electric (2021–2022, virtual)



Led the team through its most significant transition: **combustion → electric powertrain**. This required rearchitecting the entire vehicle, onboarding members to high-voltage systems, and building institutional knowledge from scratch during a COVID lockdown.

**Technical Scope**
- **Vehicle Dynamics:** Simulated cornering behavior in MATLAB & Simulink; implemented a feedback control loop for steering angle based on slip angle, yaw rate, and vehicle speed
- **Cockpit Ergonomics:** Modeled driver envelope in CATIA V5; validated cockpit geometry with a full-scale wooden mockup for driver fit and control accessibility
- **Frame:** Designed and validated the electric vehicle chassis - accounting for battery enclosure packaging, revised weight distribution, and updated crash structure requirements
- **Composites:** Extended composite usage across the new vehicle; managed in-house manufacturing for all fiber-reinforced parts

**Team & Program Management**
- Led **50+ members** across mechanical, electrical, and software subsystems
- Managed **₹3.5M budget** and relationships with **30+ sponsors**
- Coordinated fully remotely during COVID - established async workflows and design review processes to maintain momentum
